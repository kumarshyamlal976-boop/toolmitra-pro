How to use the SIP Calculator

Monthly contributions at the beginning of each month. Enter the requested values and select Calculate.

Formula and assumptions

FV = P × ((1 + r)^n − 1) / r × (1 + r), where r is annual return divided by 1200 and n is months. At zero return, FV = P × n.

Worked example

₹5,000 monthly for 10 years at zero return totals ₹6,00,000. Market returns fluctuate; this is not a forecast.
