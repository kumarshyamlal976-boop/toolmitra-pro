How to use the PPF Calculator

Illustrative annual contribution projection. Enter the requested values and select Calculate.

Formula and assumptions

Each year, add the annual contribution and multiply the balance by (1 + R/100). This assumes beginning-of-year deposits and a constant rate.

Worked example

₹10,000 annually for two years at an assumed 7% gives ₹22,149. This is a mathematical illustration, not a PPF withdrawal schedule. Actual PPF rates, deposit limits and maturity rules apply.
