How to use the EMI Calculator

Estimate a fixed-rate loan repayment. Enter the requested values and select Calculate.

Formula and assumptions

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n − 1), with monthly rate r and n monthly payments. At zero interest, EMI = P / n.

Worked example

A ₹1,20,000 loan for 12 months at zero interest costs ₹10,000 monthly. Processing fees and floating rates are excluded.
