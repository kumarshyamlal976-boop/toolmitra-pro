How to use the FD Calculator

Estimate quarterly compounded fixed deposit interest. Enter the requested values and select Calculate.

Formula and assumptions

Maturity = P × (1 + R/400)^(4t), where R is annual percentage rate and t is years.

Worked example

₹10,000 at 8% for one year grows to approximately ₹10,824.32 before tax. Actual bank conventions may differ.
