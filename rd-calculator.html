How to use the RD Calculator

Estimate monthly recurring deposits. Enter the requested values and select Calculate.

Formula and assumptions

FV = P × ((1 + r)^n − 1) / r × (1 + r), using monthly compounding and beginning-of-month deposits.

Worked example

₹1,000 monthly for one year at zero interest totals ₹12,000. Banks may use quarterly compounding and different deposit dates.
