How to use the GST Calculator

Enter the amount and GST rate. Choose Add GST if the entered amount excludes tax, or Remove GST if it already includes tax. Press Calculate to view the taxable value, GST and total.

Frequently asked questions

Is this GST calculator free?

Yes. Calculations run in your browser without an account.

How is GST removed from an inclusive amount?

Taxable value = total amount ÷ (1 + GST rate ÷ 100). GST is the difference between the total and taxable value.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Results are estimates; applicable GST rates, rounding, and compliance rules should be independently verified.
