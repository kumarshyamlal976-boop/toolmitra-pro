LEARN THE BASICS

Helpful guides

01 / FINANCE

What is a SIP?

A systematic investment plan invests a fixed amount at regular intervals. Our SIP tool estimates future value using a constant assumed return; real market returns vary and are not guaranteed.

02 / LOANS

How does EMI work?

An equated monthly installment spreads loan principal and interest across a set number of months. Actual lender charges and changing interest rates can affect repayment.

03 / TAX

Understanding GST

GST can be added to a base price or extracted from a tax-inclusive price. Confirm the applicable tax rate for your goods or services with official guidance.
