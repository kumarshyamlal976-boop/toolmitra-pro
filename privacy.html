Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ToolMitra provides browser-based calculation tools. Values you enter into our calculators are processed in your browser and are not sent to a ToolMitra server by these tools.

Our hosting provider may collect technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs to deliver the website and maintain security. Consult the privacy policy of the hosting provider selected by the site owner.

The website includes Google AdSense code. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including based on prior visits. Users can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. See how Google uses information from partner sites. Where required, consent controls must be provided before advertising is activated.

External services

Our design may request fonts from Google Fonts. This can involve connections to Google's servers. No calculator values are transmitted as part of the font request.
