How to use the Lumpsum Calculator

Estimate a single investment with annual compounding. Enter the requested values and select Calculate.

Formula and assumptions

FV = P × (1 + R/100)^t. Estimated gain is FV minus P.

Worked example

₹10,000 at 10% for two years becomes ₹12,100 before fees and taxes. Returns are not guaranteed.
